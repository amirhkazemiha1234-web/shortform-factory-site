Shortform Factory

A personal tool that prepares short vertical videos and sends them to its owner's TikTok account as drafts.

What it does

  • Writes scripts and renders short videos with narration and captions.
  • Uploads each finished video to the owner's own TikTok account as a draft, to be reviewed and posted by hand in the TikTok app.
  • Reads the account's follower and video statistics to see which videos perform well.

Who uses it

Shortform Factory is operated by one person for their own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.
