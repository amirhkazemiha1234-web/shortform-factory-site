Privacy Policy

What Shortform Factory reads from TikTok, what it does with it, and how to remove its access.

Who this applies to

Shortform Factory is a personal tool. The only TikTok account it connects to is the one its owner has authorised. It does not collect information about visitors to this website and sets no cookies.

Information read from TikTok

  • Basic profile information: account identifier, display name and avatar.
  • Account statistics: follower, following, like and video counts.
  • The list of the account's public videos and their view, like, comment and share counts.

What the tool sends to TikTok

Video files and captions, uploaded to the connected account as drafts.

How the information is used

Only to upload drafts and to report on how the account's own videos perform. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Storage

Statistics are kept on the owner's own computer. The access token that TikTok issues is held by the connection provider, Composio, and is used only to carry out the actions above.

Removing access and data

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings, Security and permissions, Manage app permissions. To ask for stored data to be deleted, use the contact link below.
